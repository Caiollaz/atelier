# Niche: vertical SaaS

Read this when the page sells software to a profession or an industry: clinic management, healthtech, pharmacy compliance, legal practice, accounting, real estate, education. These buyers are not developers; they need to see the product and believe it fits their day. The page is richer than a developer tool's page. Drawn from a study of healthtech landing pages on Behance.

## 1. Restrained or rich

| Product | Richness | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Developer tools, infrastructure, APIs | Restrained: one moment, quiet chrome, the rest of this skill as written | The buyer reads docs and code; decoration costs trust |
| Vertical SaaS, healthtech, B2B for a profession | Medium-high: the product in most sections, one field change, two or three brand devices | The buyer compares screens and needs to picture their own clinic or office in them |

## 2. Hero

| Variant | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Stacked, product below** | A label naming the audience, a two-line headline, one sentence, a primary and a secondary button, centered; a large product view rising from the bottom edge and running past it. Centering is right here because the product view anchors the composition |
| **Product with chips** | The same stack, or a split, with the product view surrounded by three to six small labelled chips (feature names with an icon), one style, slightly overlapping the frame |
| **Split** | Text left, product view right at half the width or more, in a browser or device frame (`assets/browser-frame.html`) |
| **Full-bleed photo** | A photograph of the customer's world across the whole hero, headline low on a calm area, small UI cards over the photo showing what the product does in that scene |
| **Art field** | A dark full-bleed field with one abstract mark drawn from the logo, the headline centered over it, the product right after the fold |

At 2560 (`layout.md`, wide screens) the text stays at 1200 to 1320 and the product stage grows to 1600 to 1760, on a tinted field that runs edge to edge or nearly. The hero fills the first 1440px of height with the product starting inside it. Measure the references at 2560 before deciding.

The product view is the real interface (a screenshot or careful HTML with plausible data), in light on a dark page or framed on a light one. One soft wash behind it: a tonal gradient inside the accent's hue, as a background field. Never a glow around buttons, cards or text (a hard rule in `SKILL.md`).

Headline emphasis: one system, used on every headline. Common: two tones (the first line in ink, the second in a muted tone of the same ramp), or a serif display for calm, regulated categories (health, compliance, finance).

## 3. Section recipe

| Section | Composition |
|---|---|
| Hero | Section 2 |
| Proof strip | Logos of real customers or partners with permission; skip it otherwise |
| Problem | One large statement, two tones, and up to three numbers, each with its source |
| Outcomes | A bento (2x2 or 1 plus 3): each tile one outcome with a fragment of the UI or an icon |
| Features | Two or three splits alternating sides, each with a real UI crop and three short points |
| Proof | A named professional (name, role, clinic) with a portrait or a video, with permission |
| Pricing | Compare surface: aligned rows, one recommended plan with a colored header; real prices or none |
| FAQ | Accordion |
| Close | A card with the demo form or the booking action, overlapping a dark footer |

Every section has a visual anchor: a UI crop, a diagram, a stat row or a portrait. At least two sections show the real product. At least one field change (a dark or tinted band).

## 4. Devices and palettes

Devices, two or three: the logo mark as a hub or badge, a fine dot grid, one wash in the accent's hue, device frames, thin connector lines between chips.

| Family | Page | Ink | Accent | Product |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dark warm | `#140c08` | `#f2ece6` | `#f05a14` | Light screenshots, so the product pops |
| Paper and navy | `#f8f7f5` | `#1e2633` | `#3b82f6` | Light UI on a soft wash of the accent |
| Clinical white | `#f6f9f9` | `#14302f` | `#13827a` | Light UI, teal selection |

Type: a tight grotesk for everything (letter spacing about -0.02em on headlines), or a serif display with a grotesk body when the brand should read calm and regulated.

## 5. Slop that appears in this market and stays out

| Seen in the market | Instead |
|---|---|
| Four-point sparkles and twinkles | Nothing, or a real status dot |
| "Partner" logos of AI companies or big tech, unverifiable | Real customers with permission, or no strip |
| Glossy gradient buttons with a glow; bloom in every section | A solid button; one tonal wash behind the hero product, nothing glowing |
| A wash that drifts from blue to lavender | A wash inside one hue |
| Uppercase tracked labels on every section, repeating the title | Mixed-case labels only where they add information |
| Three cards followed by a four-column icon row; every icon on a tinted square | The bento or the feature splits |
| Numbers without a source ("5 to 20%", "$100k+") | Sourced numbers, or none |
| AI-generated people presented as customers | Real customers, or no people |
| A badge glued to the center of a grid | Information in the tiles |
| A floating button in the color of WhatsApp or another channel, off the palette | The brand accent with the channel's icon |

## Checklist

- [ ] Richness chosen: restrained for developer tools, medium-high for vertical SaaS.
- [ ] The real product in the hero and in at least two more sections.
- [ ] One headline emphasis system; one wash at most, inside one hue, and nothing glowing.
- [ ] Every number and logo is real and sourced.
- [ ] None of the market slop in section 5.
